StoryMap Converter version 1.1

Designed by Tom Baker

Reads a classic Esri Story Map (Map Series, Map Journal, Cascade, Shortlist, Swipe, Map Tour and others), converts its entries into ArcGIS StoryMaps blocks, and creates the new story in your ArcGIS account.

After converting your old storymap, be sure to set its sharing setting to Private.

  1. 1Read classic story
  2. 2Convert to StoryMaps
  3. 3Create new story

Works with stories on ArcGIS Online and with self-hosted classic stories on any website, such as https://storymaps.esri.com/stories/2014/womeningis/

Include in text file:
Private or ArcGIS Enterprise story
Self-hosted story on a website that blocks reading

Some websites don't let other pages read their files. Open the story in your browser and save it (Ctrl+S, "Webpage, Complete"). Then choose the saved page together with its config script, usually Config.js or config.js in the saved "_files" folder. If the config script isn't there, open its address directly in your browser and save it too.
